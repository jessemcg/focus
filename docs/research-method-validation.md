## Limits and adoption

Deterministic/offline acceptance is **not live-model comparative answer-quality
validation**. Loss of specialty shortcuts is mitigated by record-derived vocabulary,
generic contemporaneous-evidence instructions and invariant retrieval fixtures, not
hidden profiles. Live-model quality testing requires separate authorization.

Restart Focus and explicitly ask a new question to use the fixed method, even for
previously profile-bound bundles. No confirmation dialog, migration flag, private
cleanup or RecordPrep regeneration. Old saved answers retain historical metadata.
Older Focus releases may not read new schema-3 answers; never auto-down-convert them.
